KishiAri Afterword


i had joined the project about half its life ago and the original game meant a lot to me for on my 19th birthday it was my escape after a heated moment and the game was so cute and fun and novel and stuff. so of course when i learned about the translation project for the jp-exclusive 3rd game, of course i had to contribute my artistic abilities! ended up doing mostly text stuff and demoing graphics ideas for the team during its slow years. i also ended up being a manual file converter at one point.

i also find myself feeling kinda glad that, while no longer solely my contribution, my favorite work will be the one that everyone will see constantly during the dialogue =^v^=

all this to say that i hope everyone enjoys the efforts of myself and the rest of the team as much as i will pleasure myself eating this fish.
