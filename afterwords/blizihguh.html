Blizihguh Afterword


Thanks for playing Summon Night: A Swordcraft Story 3! Whether you've been waiting since 2005, or you've just discovered the game today, I hope you enjoy it.

I first discovered the series in the mid 2010s, when I was still in high school. At that time, the translation project -- this very same one, including some of the same people -- was ongoing. A decade later, I'm so proud to be part of the team as we finish up.

As I write this, I'm still in the middle of script revisions. The "translation" part of the translation has been complete for a few months, so we wanted to get it out there ASAP. At the same time, I really want this translation to be the best it can be. I believe this game deserves a professional-quality translation, and I'm doing my best to push it that extra mile. So I hope you'll take another look once that final version comes out.

I want to give a special thanks to everybody who pushed hard on this project. A lot of people put in incredible work here. Over the past few years, I had a lot of fun, learned a lot, and got to meet some really great people. As a translator, a programmer, and an artist, it's been incredible to meet so many passionate and likeminded people. I especially want to thank CornStarch, Rielinne, and Kuroba. I'm not sure this project would ever have finished without them. Seeing you all work so hard on this has been a huge inspiration!

I had a blast working on this project. I hope you have a blast playing, too :)
